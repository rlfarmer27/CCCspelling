Session

Pretest

Listen, then spell the word. You will not see it first, and you will not be told if it is right.

Step 1 · Look

Study the spelling. Say it in your head. When you are ready, cover it and write it from memory.

Step 2 · Write

Write the word from memory. The model is hidden.

Step 3 · Compare

Look at the model and your spelling side by side. Check one letter at a time. Then decide for yourself.
